# Support

## Questions about this documentation

Open an issue on `bitcoinuniverseio/src-20`. Include the page and the section heading you are asking about.

## Questions about the SRC-20 protocol itself

SRC-20 originated in the Bitcoin Stamps community, not in Bitcoin Universe. Protocol questions are best answered by the people who maintain the indexers:

- Bitcoin Stamps indexer: <https://github.com/stampchain-io/btc_stamps>
- Stampchain explorer and API: <https://stampchain.io/>

## Questions about Bitcoin Universe products

The central documentation portal is <https://docs.bitcoinuniverse.io>. For a specific product, use the issue tracker of that product's repository.

## Suspected vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What we cannot help with

We cannot recover funds, reverse a broadcast Bitcoin transaction, un-mint a token, or change how an independent indexer interprets your transaction. If a transaction confirmed but the token result was not what you expected, check the transaction in a Stamps explorer first: the transaction and the token interpretation are two separate layers.
