A simple journey

Four steps to a confident experience.

Every action ends with a real network transaction. Take the time to understand what your wallet shows.

01

Choose your world

Start with a Stamp, an SRC-20 asset, or an SRC-101 name—each has its own purpose and flow.

02

Explore the history

Review the creator, supply or namespace, prior activity, and the service presenting it.

03

Inspect the transaction

Confirm network, recipient, amount, outputs, miner fee, and any service fee before signing.

04

Verify the result

After confirmation, follow the asset or name in a compatible Bitcoin Stamps explorer.

Stay in control

Your keys. Your review. Your decision.

Bitcoin confirms the transaction; compatible Stamps services interpret asset and name activity. Never share a seed phrase, never blind-sign, and always verify recipients, amounts, outputs, and fees.
