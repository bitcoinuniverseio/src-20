## What makes it special

- **Stamps:** Discover art and culture preserved through Bitcoin transaction history.
- **SRC-20:** Explore fungible assets with deploy, mint, and transfer activity.
- **SRC-101:** Build identity through namespaces, unique names, renewals, and records.

## A clear onchain promise

Stamps gives creators and collectors a durable canvas. SRC-20 adds community tokens and SRC-101 adds names—each with a distinct lifecycle interpreted by compatible Stamps services.

## Stay in control

Bitcoin confirms the transaction; compatible Stamps services interpret asset and name activity. Never share a seed phrase, never blind-sign, and always verify recipients, amounts, outputs, and fees.
