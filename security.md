# Stay safe with Stamps, SRC-20, and SRC-101

Onchain culture is exciting because you stay in control. Keep that control by slowing down for the few details that matter most.

## Protect your wallet

- Never share a seed phrase, recovery phrase, or private key.
- Use a wallet you trust and approve only transactions you understand.
- Ignore unsolicited “support” messages asking you to connect, sign, or move funds.
- Confirm that the site and wallet prompt match the experience you intended to use.

## Review before signing

Check the Bitcoin network, recipient, asset or name, amount, transaction outputs, miner fee, and any clearly disclosed service fee. If any detail looks unfamiliar, cancel and start again.

SRC-20 assets and SRC-101 names are interpreted by compatible Bitcoin Stamps services. A confirmed Bitcoin transaction does not by itself guarantee the asset or name result you expected, so follow the result in a compatible explorer after confirmation.

## Know what you are collecting

Ticker symbols and names can look alike. Review the asset’s or namespace’s history, creator information, and transaction identity rather than relying on a familiar word or logo alone.

External links and social profiles are not proof of identity. Open links carefully and verify destinations before connecting a wallet.

## Give confirmations time

Network activity can take time to settle. Keep the transaction ID, wait for confirmation, and verify the final Stamps, SRC-20, or SRC-101 result before taking another action.

The golden rule is simple: your keys, your review, your decision.
